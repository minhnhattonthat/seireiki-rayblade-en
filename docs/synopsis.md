# Synopsis - Seireiki Rayblade (for translators and reviewers)

Built 2026-11-03 from three readers' passes over a sampled digest (every ~14th line of every scene) plus the DC fan
project's character notes (`work/glossary_seed.tsv`). Scene numbers are D_SCE slots (the `sceNNN` in ids); slot order
is not strictly story order and many scenes are route/heroine variants. "Unclear" = the digest didn't show it.
The extracted script decides the wording; this file only tells you who is who and what is coming.

## Premise
Touya Kazami, a brusque Japanese high-school student whose parents died (his cat Yamato, now able to talk, lives with
him), is pulled into Agarthia, a world that branched off from Earth ~4000 years ago. There he pilots the Spirit Mech
Rayblade for the Liebhofen (a landship crew built around the Holy Land's Spirit Mech project) against Ze=Ode, the
group of Dr. Debish, whose experiment 109 years ago caused the "Ze=Ode Terror" explosion; a similar reaction 3 years
ago is why Earth people (Touya, Ai, Kasumi, Clovis, Arthur, Xiujian, Melissa, Aurelie...) are being pulled across.
Free time between battles is the Private Phase: talk events, Topics and Affection with the heroines (Ai, Kasumi,
Celica, Ymir, Aurelie, Melvi, Leone, Kururu, Melissa); the ending depends on the chosen heroine.

## Plot by scene group
- 000: Yamato's Affection/Topic tutorial lines.
- 001-002: Prologue. Classroom (teacher Ayaka, friend Koichiro, childhood friend Kasumi). A giant robot appears;
  Touya climbs in. Forzen (Holy Land alchemist, cheerfully evasive, pushes contracts) explains Agarthia, Ze=Ode and the
  "six" people like Touya. Scene 002 head is a dev chapter-select menu.
- 003-009: York (queen Alforina) is invaded by the Zigrim Republic (Reamond, Fores); Alforina refuses to surrender and
  later dies (mourned; Ai takes it hard). Lodis, Celica and Tix (Agarthian royals) are recalled. Murk's lab loses power
  and units escape. The Holy Land, Rayblade sealed there, its key the Resonance Crystal in York. Oswald (York
  chancellor) is scheming; he later asks to be called "Clime" in disguise. Reduce trains Touya with a sword. Dei (council
  chair), Doris, Melvi appear; simulator training.
- 010-025: Maki (reporter) sneaks aboard; Revios and Geel plot. City outing (Selg, Rosalie, Growster, Lyle). Leone
  appears looking for her father. Vales's Solitary Silver Wings (Jono, Vel); Baio and Bio; Cain. Sharl introduces
  herself; Debish returns ("109 years later"), taking Dei's place; Valga wakes sleepers. Cornel Village hot-spring
  peeping farce. Maki finds the Ze=Ode base; Leone appears brainwashed.
- 026-047: Touya in a dream void, wakes in Rayblade two days later. Zigrim cooperates for a time. Leone: the
  Zelbario stole her will. Miyasco and Reamond are Code 3 subjects (Reamond is dying). Horem (Zigrim president) is
  killed by his adjutant. Rishuna (from Lin Karma): the true threat is Vorlia, not Ze=Ode; if it revives, people
  vanish. Rayfon is alive, bonded with Grauzer (Aidion). Xiujian trains Touya; Lyle duels Touya; Bernst visits
  unarmed and hints at Rayblade's origin. Final assault on Debish; Wenimas explains the two-seater.
- 048-079: Clime unmasked as Oswald; Steinies hunts Thora/Kururu; Alforina's funeral. Final dungeon variants inside
  Vorlia: the Voice (an Estelian entity that treats humanity as entertainment, "a wager") speaks in 声（...）
  lines; Black Rayblade. Each variant has a different partner heroine; Leone fades in Touya's arms (065). 066-073:
  endings on Earth per heroine. 074-079: alternate route (Cain/Selg-led coup, Cain dies in Rayfon's arms).
- 080-127, 186-190, 219-230, 256-298, 362-446: Private Phase events (heroine talks, Fain/Derock elevator gags,
  cooking, training, lore lectures by Derock and Arthur, Melvi lost in a storeroom). Many begin with the dev guard
  line 「1B0のフラグが立っていません…」 (translate it plainly; players never see it).
- 128-129: battle tutorial; an alternate opening (Koichiro, Yamato, Forzen's contract).
- 130-185: Dawn of an Era terrorists (Render, Barkin, Resh, Doris; Barkin dies naming the orphans Demi, Rii, Rudi);
  Liebhofen crew (Miyasco = operator and doctor, Captain Roa says little but "Mm."). Lyle (Rayode) is Ymir's father and
  dies (153). Baio uses an amnesiac Melissa as test pilot (she calls him 博士 "Doctor"); Xiujian vows to save her.
  Capital terror attack: Lodis wounded, Marshal Revios dies protecting the King (Growster mourns his rival). Gen's
  father is Vales. Clovis's wife died ("You're free"). Sharl calls Rayblade the sixth Ze=Ode.
- 192-255: Flamuelk Castle falls; Oswald turns traitor; Ciel's "revenge list"; the Holy Land ball; Eleana vanishes;
  Leone gives Touya her treasure; Ymir is to become Matriarch; Agarthia claims the Spirit Mechs; Garna dies holding
  the York fort; Murk built Baluamy (Gazette Project). Scenes 239 and 252 are dev face/BGM test menus.
- 336-346: Zigrim asks for a ceasefire; Rayfon may leave. Ivel reveals Rayblade is a Ze=Ode. Leone wakes with no
  memory; the Mysterious Figure in a Bine Daga is Baio. Assault on Debish's base (Ryord dies; Garyud holds the line);
  Debish wants a world without love, the heroines answer; Debish dies.
- 347-361: endings (Touya wakes in class on Friday April 8 again; Wenimas thanks him and explains parallel worlds)
  and the Vorlia final dungeon per heroine; post-war party (Ciel becomes queen of York).
- 368-383: York liberation; Grad (Zigrim, "Lion of the West") fights to save Thora and Kururu; Horem admits the York
  invasion and the plot on Alforina; Ciel learns she has royal blood (Garyud raised her); Debish resurgent; Sharl
  abducts Ymir; Wenimas (now disembodied) tells Touya she summoned him for his strong Prana.

## Who is who (reveals: don't give them away early)

## Voices at a glance (details in docs/style-reference.md)
Yamato replaces な-sounds with ニャ; Derock ends lines with なんだな; Fain is bombastic ("Dahahaha! Justice!");
Murk, Valga, Growster, Garyud, Baio and the Mysterious Figure are old-man ワシ/じゃ; Gen uses オイラ, calls Touya
兄ちゃん; Melvi talks about herself in the third person, calls Touya お兄ちゃん; Leone uses ボク; Aurelie アタシ;
Kasumi calls him トウヤちゃん; Reduce is archaic (かたじけない); Sisk wails 殿下ぁ～; Bernst is over-polite and
menacing; Roa says little (うむ).
